Questions

Good to know.

What exactly is a synbiotic?
A synbiotic combines prebiotics (fiber that feeds good bacteria), probiotics (the live beneficial strains) and postbiotics (their beneficial by-products) in one formula — so every root+ stick supports your microbiome from multiple angles.
How do I take it?
One 3g sachet a day, poured directly onto the tongue. No water, no mixing, no shaker — take it anywhere, anytime as part of your daily ritual.
What's the CFU count?
Goodnight and Skin Radiance are formulated to deliver a total of 30 billion CFU per serving, at clinically meaningful, fully-disclosed doses. Balance leads with Pylopass® at 200mg plus B. lactis Bl-04® and TCI711.
Is it third-party tested, vegan and non-GMO?
Yes. Every formula is backed by COA / lab verification with guaranteed CFU, and made to clean, non-GMO, vegan-friendly standards. No proprietary blends — you see exactly what's inside and how much.
